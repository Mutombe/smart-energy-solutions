FAQ

Before you|get in touch

Is the site survey really free?

Yes. Surveys in and around Harare are free with no obligation. For remote sites we may agree a small travel fee, which is credited back if you go ahead.

How much does a home system cost?

It depends on what you want to keep running. Try the savings calculator for an indicative size and payback. Your survey gives a fixed, itemised price.

How long does installation take?

Most homes take one to three days on site. Commercial systems typically take two to eight weeks, depending on size and approvals.

What warranties do I get?

Panels typically carry 12–15 year product and 25–30 year performance warranties. Inverters and batteries usually carry 5–10 years, and we add our own workmanship warranty on every install.

Do you work outside Harare?

Yes. We deliver projects nationwide, from Bulawayo and Mutare to farms and lodges in remote areas.
