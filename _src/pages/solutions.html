Questions

Good questions|we hear a lot

Can’t see yours? Ask an engineer.

Will solar work during load-shedding?

Yes, if the system has a hybrid inverter and batteries. Grid-tied systems without storage shut down during outages for safety. Every home system we design includes backup for your essential circuits.

How long do the batteries last?

We use lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO₄) cells rated for 6,000+ cycles, which is typically 10 to 15 years of daily use. Old lead-acid batteries usually last two to four years.

What happens on cloudy or rainy days?

Panels still produce at reduced output, and your battery and the grid (or generator) top up the rest automatically. We size systems around Zimbabwe’s rainy-season irradiance, not just the sunny months.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We offer deposit-and-instalment plans for homes and work with partner banks on asset finance for businesses and farms. Ask for options during your survey.

Can you service a system someone else installed?

Absolutely. Our care plans include an inspection and health report on third-party systems, followed by any repairs or upgrades needed to bring them up to standard.
